Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

This policy covers nickknows.net and the personal projects that sign in with Google through it. They are hobby projects run by one person, not a business. Nothing here is sold, rented, or used for advertising.

Signing in with Google

Projects that let you sign in with Google receive your name, email address, and profile picture. They are used only to sign you in and to show who is signed in. Nothing else about your Google account is requested.

Gmail (read-only)

One private project, a personal finance dashboard used only by its owner, asks for read-only Gmail access. It searches for bill reminder emails from specific billers (for example, a utility's "upcoming bill" email) and reads the amount due and due date from them. It cannot send, change, or delete email. The access token is stored encrypted on a private server, and the email contents it reads are not shared with anyone.

Google user data

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Removing access

You can revoke any of these projects' access to your Google account at any time from your Google account permissions. To have stored data deleted, or with any question about this policy, email nickco7@gmail.com.
